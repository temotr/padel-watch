Sunday Tandem 15.11
Sunday 15 November at 17:00 - 19:30 (Austria) / Sunday 15 November at 16:00 UTC - 18:30 UTC (Local)
Padeldome Erdberg
Sunday Tandem is our fixed-pairs format: one partner, one team, the whole evening. There's no rotation and no reshuffling — you and your partner stay together for every single match, building rhythm, finding your positions and figuring out how to win as a team. Pairs are grouped by the average rating of the two players, so every match stays balanced. The top 20 pairs start at 17:00 and all other pairs at 17:30, and the full schedule is published in advance, so you'll know exactly when and where you play. Already have a partner? Register and write their name in the comments — we'll put you together. Don't have a partner yet? Register anyway and leave us a note in the comments; we'll match you with someone at a similar level before the schedule goes out.

🕵️Padel Mafia 19.11
Thursday 19 November at 18:00 - 20:00 (Austria) / Thursday 19 November at 17:00 UTC - 19:00 UTC (Local)
The Dome Stadlau
16 players, 2 secret Mafias, and nobody knows who they are. Padel Mafia mixes King of the Court with the classic party game. Two players get a secret mafia role at the start, and their mission is to sabotage the games without getting caught, while everyone else tries to spot them. 🎾🎭 How it works 16 players, 2 Mafias. Roles are handed out secretly before the first game. Play and observe. Was that a bad bandeja, or was it on purpose? 👀 The vote. At the end of the event, everyone votes for who they think the Mafias are. If a Mafia is caught, their games are counted normally and they get the points they actually earned. If a Mafia stays hidden, their game points are flipped into negative, and all other players get positive points. Reduced stakes: Elo points for this event count 25% less than the default rate, so you can bluff, suspect and laugh without risking your ranking too much.
